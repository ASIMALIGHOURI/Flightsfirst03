Questions about flying with Vietnam Airlines

Does Vietnam Airlines fly direct from London?

Yes. Vietnam Airlines flies from London Heathrow to Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Other Vietnamese cities connect via those two.

Which UK airport does Vietnam Airlines use?

London Heathrow. Your agent confirms current schedules for your dates.

What is the baggage allowance?

Vietnam Airlines lists a free 23 kg checked bag in Economy and 32 kg in Business. Your agent confirms it on your quote.

Are you Vietnam Airlines?

No. Flightsfirst is an independent travel agency run in the UK. We are not Vietnam Airlines and this is not the airline's official website.

Is there a booking fee?

Yes. We charge £25 per passenger for Economy and £50 per passenger for Business Class on top of the airline fare. Your agent confirms the fare, taxes, booking fee and total price before you pay.

Will I get a confirmation after booking?

Yes. Once your booking is complete you receive your booking confirmation and e-ticket by email.

How quickly will someone call me back?

Usually within minutes during the day. If you would rather talk now, call 0203 981 0232. Lines are open 24/7.

Can I hold a seat with a deposit?

Often yes, depending on the fare and the airline's rules. Your agent confirms the deposit amount and how long the seat is held before you pay anything.
