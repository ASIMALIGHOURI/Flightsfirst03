Fares shown on this website or quoted by phone, WhatsApp or email are per person unless stated otherwise and are subject to availability at the time of booking. 4. Fees and Total Price

We charge a booking fee for arranging your flights: £25 per passenger for Economy and £50 per passenger for Business Class. The fee is in addition to the airline fare and taxes. We will tell you the booking fee and the total price, including airline taxes and all charges, before you pay, and we do not add any other charges afterwards.

If a separate fee applies to a particular service, such as a change or a cancellation, we will explain it before you confirm that service. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.

5. Making a Booking

Bookings are made through our travel consultants by phone, WhatsApp, email or callback. A booking is confirmed once we have received full payment (or an agreed deposit, where applicable) and issued your confirmation. Please check every name, date and detail on your confirmation as soon as you receive it and tell us immediately about any error.

6. Payment

Payment is taken securely over the phone at the time of booking by debit or credit card, as confirmed by your travel consultant. Any balance must be paid by the date we give you at the time of booking. We do not store full card details after your payment has been processed. Prices are quoted in GBP unless stated otherwise.

It is your responsibility to make sure that you and everyone travelling with you hold valid passports, any visas required and meet any health or entry requirements for your destination and any transit points. Many countries require a passport to be valid for at least six months from your date of entry, so please check the rules for your destination with the relevant embassy or consulate. We can offer general guidance, but responsibility rests with the traveller, and we are not liable for losses caused by incorrect or missing travel documents.

8. Passenger Names and Corrections

The name on each ticket must match the passenger's passport exactly. Corrections after ticketing may be refused by the airline or may incur airline fees, so please check carefully before you confirm your booking.

9. Baggage and Airline Rules

Baggage allowances, seat selection, meals and other services are set by the airline and can vary by route, cabin and fare type. We will tell you the allowance that applies to your fare when you book, but the airline's own conditions of carriage apply to your journey and take priority.

10. Changes by You or by Us

If you need to change a confirmed booking, contact us as early as possible. Changes are subject to the airline's fare rules and may involve change fees, a fare difference, or both, and some fares cannot be changed. Airlines sometimes change schedules or cancel flights outside our control. If this happens we will tell you as soon as we are notified and explain your options.
