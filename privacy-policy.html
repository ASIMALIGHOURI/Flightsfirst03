1. Who We Are

Flightsfirst.co.uk is an independent travel agency and a trading name of Panama Travels Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), registered in the United Kingdom with its registered office at 495 Coventry Road, Small Heath, Birmingham, B10 0JS. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information when you contact us, request a quote, or make a booking.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as when you call, WhatsApp, submit a callback request, or make a booking. This may include:

  • Full name, contact number, email address and postal address
  • Travel preferences, dates, destinations and passenger details
  • Passport and travel document details required to complete a booking
  • Payment details, processed securely at the time of booking
  • Records of calls, WhatsApp messages and correspondence with our team

We also automatically collect limited technical data when you visit our website, such as your device type, browser, approximate location and the pages you view, using cookies and similar technologies. If you arrive via a Google Ads advert, we may also capture the Google Click Identifier (GCLID) associated with that click and include it with an enquiry you submit, so we can measure which adverts lead to genuine bookings. See our Cookie Policy for details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide flight quotes and process bookings on your behalf
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or booking by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email
  • To arrange ATOL protection and issue certificates where applicable
  • To respond to a callback request submitted through this website
  • To comply with our legal, regulatory and financial obligations
  • To improve our website, services and Google Ads marketing performance (in aggregated, non-identifying form wherever possible)

4. Our Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under UK GDPR on the following bases: performance of a contract (arranging and managing your booking), our legitimate interests (responding to enquiries, improving our service, preventing fraud), and, where required, your consent (for example, for certain marketing communications, which you can withdraw at any time).

5. Who We Share Your Information With

To arrange your travel, we may share relevant details with airlines, ATOL-bonded suppliers, payment processors and other third parties directly involved in fulfilling your booking. We may also share information with regulatory bodies such as the Civil Aviation Authority where required, and with IT and telephony providers who support our operations under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including any legal, accounting or reporting requirements — typically for the duration of your booking plus a period afterwards required for financial and regulatory record-keeping.

7. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to request data portability. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to support essential functionality and to help us understand how visitors use our site, including via Google Ads and analytics tools. Full details are set out in our Cookie Policy.

9. Keeping Your Data Secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Payment details are handled through secure, industry-standard payment processes and are not stored in full by our team.
