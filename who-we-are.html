About Us

An Independent UK Travel Agency, Built Around a Phone Call

We started Flightsfirst.co.uk with one belief: booking flights should feel like speaking to a knowledgeable friend, not fighting a website. Here's who we are and how we work.

Independent, Human-First and ATOL Protected

Flightsfirst.co.uk is an independent travel agency serving customers across the UK. We're not an airline, and we're not a faceless comparison engine — we're a dedicated team of flight booking specialists who search, negotiate and confirm fares for you, over the phone, WhatsApp or via a simple callback request.

Because we operate independently of any single airline, we're free to compare across multiple carriers and routings to find the combination that genuinely suits your budget and travel dates, rather than steering you toward one preferred supplier.

What We Do

  • Search live fares across a wide range of airlines and routes worldwide
  • Match flexible travel dates to find the cheapest days to fly
  • Hold and confirm bookings securely over the phone
  • Issue ATOL certificates and e-tickets by email once a booking is complete
  • Provide ongoing support before and after you travel

ATOL & IATA Accreditation

Where a flight-inclusive booking qualifies, it is protected under Air Travel Organiser's Licence (ATOL) number 3781, held by our parent company, Panama Travels Ltd. This means that, in the unlikely event of a failure, the ATOL scheme is there to help with a refund or getting you home if you're already abroad. You'll always receive an ATOL Certificate confirming exactly what's protected.

Panama Travels Ltd is also an IATA accredited agency. IATA (the International Air Transport Association) accreditation is granted only to agencies that meet strict financial and operational standards, giving airlines and travellers confidence in how bookings are ticketed and managed. Flightsfirst operates under Panama Travels Ltd, so every booking you make with us carries the backing of both accreditations.

Flightsfirst.co.uk & Panama Travels Ltd

Flightsfirst.co.uk is a trading name of Panama Travels Ltd, a UK registered company based at 495 Coventry Road, Small Heath, Birmingham, B10 0JS, and an ATOL & IATA accredited company.
