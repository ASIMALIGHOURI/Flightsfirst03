Questions about flying to Harare

Is the London Gatwick to Harare flight really direct?

Yes. Air Zimbabwe flies nonstop between London Gatwick (LGW) and Harare (HRE). The journey takes about 10 hours with no stop and no change of aircraft.

Which days does Air Zimbabwe fly from Gatwick to Harare?

Currently on Monday, Thursday and Saturday from Gatwick South Terminal. Schedules can change, so your agent confirms exact times before you pay.

What is the baggage allowance?

Air Zimbabwe allows 2 pieces of 23 kg each in Economy and 3 pieces of 23 kg each in Business on international flights, plus 10 kg of cabin baggage. Your agent confirms it on your quote before you pay.
